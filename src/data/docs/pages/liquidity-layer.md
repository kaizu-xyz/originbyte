# Liquidity Layer

The NFT Liquidity Layer is a Protocol and ecosystem designed to share liquidity and power market-based features for institutional and retail users alike. As a public good, our partners can compose with our on-chain central limit `orderbook` and various auction types to aggregate NFT trading into a single point, across the entire chain.

## Trading Contracts

Currently, our liquidity layer offers two trading contracts:

- [NFT Orderbook](/docs/liquidity-layer/orderbook)
- [Bidding Contract](/docs/liquidity-layer/bidding)
